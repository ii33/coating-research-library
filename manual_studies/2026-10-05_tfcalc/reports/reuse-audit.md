## Architecture recommendation, not an implemented change

Introduce a GUI-independent film-design domain: immutable stack/material snapshots with explicit substrate/backside/coherence/unit/convention fields; pure vectorized optical evaluator; observable transforms; target/merit service; optimizer/tolerance service; serializable result plus warnings and evidence scope. Keep research module dependencies and product deposition-rate physics separate. Product optical datasets are the material-provider boundary, with explicit decision required when k is unknown. Do not import research `ROOT` loaders/vendor copies wholesale.

Expose future TaskService adapter around this domain rather than embedding loops in QML. Estimate workload from wavelength × angle × polarization × layers × scenarios × iterations; cap/cache/chunk, report measured progress, support cooperative cancellation, preserve request digest and algorithm/material hashes. Product `core/compute_tasks.py` already supplies `ComputeTaskStore`, input digests and compact queue/progress receipts; there is no `TaskService` class found in the inspected product copy. Its currently enumerated short-compute kinds do not include film design: extending task kind/resource policies requires deliberate implementation and validation. Existing library UI immutable-snapshot/cancel tests are patterns to preserve.
