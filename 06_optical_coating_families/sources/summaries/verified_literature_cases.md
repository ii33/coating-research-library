# Verified primary coating cases

Checked 2026-10-02. Primary-paper facts below are compact paraphrases, not copied papers. No original PDFs were saved. Search-indexed full text was used where publisher fetches failed. Layer thickness means physical thickness only where explicitly identified; optical-thickness multipliers must not be relabeled as nanometres. “Absent” means not established by the accessible evidence, not proof that the full paper omits it.

## 1. Dual-band MWIR/LWIR AR on Ge and ZnSe

**Rahmlow et al., “Dual band antireflection coatings for the infrared,” SPIE 6940, 69400T (2008).** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.780288); [author/vendor-hosted paper at Corning](https://www.corning.com/content/dam/corning/media/worldwide/csm/documents/Dual_band_antireflection__Rahmlow.pdf)

- **Locators:** pp. 5–7, Tables 1–3; Figures 22–25
- **Materials/disclosure:** ZnS/YF3 or Ge/ZnS/YF3. Table 1 gives layer counts and total physical thickness, not individual layers. Example three-material ZnSe design: 15 layers, 6.03 µm; film-only modeled averages 99.53% / 99.59%
- **Measured:** Table 3 gives complete-window mean T at normal incidence of 97.3% / 96.8% (Ge), and 98.0% / 97.6% (ZnSe), for 3.5–5 / 7.8–10.5 µm. At 30°: Ge 95.9% / 96.3%; ZnSe 97.9% / 99.0%
- **Boundary conditions:** 25.4 mm diameter, 1 mm thick witnesses; both surfaces coated; absorption included. Reflectance measured at 10°, transmission at 0° and 30°. Polarization and environmental qualification absent
- **Caution:** Table 2 is prediction, Table 3 measurement. Table 3 does not clearly assign its ZnSe row to the two- versus three-material sample. Do not assume a full disclosed stack or apply these bands to 8–14 µm

## 2. Heat-resistant LWIR AR on silicon

**Papatzacos et al., “Temperature resistant anti-reflective coating on Si-wafer for long-wave infra-red imaging,” Heliyon 9, e15888 (2023).** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e15888); [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10172777/); [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2405844023030955)

- **Locators:** Table 1; §§1.1–1.3; Figures 2, 4, 5
- **Disclosed stack, substrate outward:** Si / MgO 1–2 nm / ZnS 1180 nm / YF3 1908 nm / air, using Table 1 batch 2–4 values; adhesion layer is described separately
- **Source inconsistency:** prose instead states 1183/1935 nm and 1.04-quarter-wave layers at 10 µm. Preserve this discrepancy; use Table 1 as the explicitly labeled fabrication-composition source rather than silently mixing values
- **Measured:** successful one-sided coating improved 8–12 µm average transmission by >15% at 0° and 13% at 30° versus bare wafer, per conclusion; these are improvement claims, not absolute T. Figure 5 additionally compares two-sided coating
- **Conditions:** 550 µm-thick, 100 mm-diameter Si; e-beam evaporation; 100°C deposition; 300°C for 1 h without observed cracking/delamination or spectral change; FTIR 4 cm−1 resolution, 16 scans
- **Absent:** polarization-resolved acceptance, long-duration environmental lifetime, full uncertainty budget

## 3. 45° NIR/SWIR-reflecting, MWIR/LWIR-transmitting dichroic

**Jiang et al., “NIR to LWIR Dichroic Beamsplitter Designed and Manufactured for Space Optical Remote Sensor,” Coatings 14, 235 (2024).** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/coatings14020235); [publisher](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-6412/14/2/235); [accessible same-paper PDF](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/77ca/6ebbf60852b68fc67545874afabb7da1ea27.pdf)

- **Locators:** Table 1 (rear AR), Table 2/Figure 5 (model), Table 3/Figure 6 (measurement), §2.3 (environment)
- **Rear-AR layer numbers 1–9, material:physical-nm:** ZnS:89.5; Ge:201.3; ZnS:281.2; Ge:106.4; ZnS:149.3; YbF3:138.5; ZnS:521.6; YbF3:975.9; ZnS:50.2. Table labels design thickness; verify orientation before computational reuse
- **Front:** Ge/ZnS plus ZnS/YbF3 reflection stacks and matching layers; complete final front thickness prescription absent
- **Measured at 45°:** mean R=95%,97%,96.5% over 1.28–1.38,1.58–1.83,1.95–2.32 µm; mean T=92% over both 3.7–6.2 and 7.5–12.5 µm. These are lower than design values
- **Conditions:** single-crystal Ge; rear AR included; deposition 200°C. Tested water 45°C/12 h, tape pull, −50/60°C (2 h, three repetitions), 47°C/95%RH/24 h. Polarization and substrate thickness absent in verified text
- **Caution:** does not establish 14 µm throughput or non-polarizing performance

## 4. Protected silver mirror: explicit metal/protection recipe

**Boccas et al., “Protected-silver coatings for the 8-m Gemini telescope mirrors,” Thin Solid Films 502, 275–280 (2006).** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsf.2005.07.295); [publisher primary text](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S004060900501165X)

- **Locators:** §1.3 (recipe/feasibility), §§3.1–3.3, Figures 3–5 (spectra), §4 (durability)
- **Substrate→air physical stack:** NiCrNx 5 nm / Ag 200 nm / NiCrNx 0.8 nm / SiNx 15 nm
- **Measured distinction:** 99.1% R at 10 µm belongs to earlier feasibility/demo production. Current protected-Ag films were about 0.4 percentage point below that figure. Do not label 99.1% the demonstrated performance of every Gemini production mirror
- **Measured emission:** fresh four-layer coating emissivity 1.2% at 3.8 µm, versus 0.6% bare Ag and 2.6% Al
- **Conditions:** magnetron-sputtered front-surface coatings; spectra 0.3–20 µm. MIR sample compartment was unpurged, retaining atmospheric spectral features. Witness durability included exposed 30×30 cm float-glass samples; accelerated tests included humidity/temperature, salt fog, tape, abrasion and H2S
- **Absent:** wavelength-resolved polarization/AOI conditions in accessible excerpt, complete dispersion, guaranteed lifetime. Back-surface transmission is not the optical channel of this opaque mirror

## 5. Wide-angle 1064 nm dielectric HR

**Zhu et al., “Development of Wide-Angle Depolarizing Reflector at 1064 nm,” Materials 16, 4258 (2023).** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma16124258); [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10303084/); [supplement locator](https://www.mdpi.com/article/10.3390/ma16124258/s1)

- **Locators:** Table 1; §3.1/Figure 4 (design), §3.4/Figure 7 (measured); Supplementary Table S1 (per-layer physical thickness)
- **Materials/disclosure:** Ta2O5/SiO2 on BK7; 55 layers, approximately 9.41 µm total physical thickness. Supplementary Table S1 is explicitly identified as the complete layer-thickness source, but its values were not retrieved in this pass. Do not reconstruct the 55-layer prescription from the short inline formula
- **Measured:** Cary 7000; average R>99.5% with |Rp−Rs|<1 percentage point in 1064±40 nm for AOI 0–60°, according to §3.4. Figure 4's higher numerical values are modeled
- **Interpretation:** “depolarizing” here denotes reduced s/p reflectance separation; the paper does not establish randomization of polarization state
- **Absent:** verified back coating, substrate thickness, full environmental qualification and a usable retrieved S1 prescription

## 6. 45° LWIR thin-film polarizing beamsplitter

**Liu et al., “High-reliability infrared broadband thin-film polarizing beam splitter with ZnSe compensation layers,” Optics Express 32, 10910–10924 (2024).** [DOI/primary text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.515751)

- **Locators:** §§2.2–2.3; §3.3, Figure 7, Table 4; measured characterization later in §3
- **Materials/geometry:** Ge(H)/ZnSe(N)/YbF3(L), 30 mm diameter ×3 mm ZnSe substrate, 45° operation; rear AR included
- **Selected design:** 33 physical layers, 14.5 µm total; substrate→air optical coefficients: 0.967H 0.494N 0.6L 0.342N [0.5H 0.2N 0.6L 0.2N 0.5H]^6 0.558N 0.599L 0.5N 0.52H; reference wavelength 8.33 µm. Adjacent H half-layers merge. These coefficients are NOT physical thicknesses; full per-layer nm values and rear-AR prescription not retrieved
- **Measured:** Tp>96% and Tp/Ts>100 within 10.6±0.15 µm, 45°. The 367 nm bandwidth in Figure 7b is a design result
- **Conditions:** 200±1°C deposition; FTIR. Tests: tape; water 45°C/8 h; 45°C/95%RH/24 h; three cycles at 50/−40°C, 1 h each
- **Caution:** spectral extinction does not by itself establish phase retardance, wavefront or laser-damage performance

## Additional high-value AR source (optional substitution)

**Yu et al., “Ultrathin Ge-YF3 antireflective coating with 0.5% reflectivity on high-index substrate for long-wavelength infrared cameras,” Nanophotonics 13, 4067–4078 (2024).** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/nanoph-2024-0360); [publisher full text](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1515/nanoph-2024-0360)

- **Locators:** §2 immediately before Figure 4; Figures 3–5; §§4.2–4.3
- **Disclosed optimized sequence:** Ge 80 / YF3 1080 / Ge 348 / YF3 205 / Ge 680 nm, total 2393 nm (design). Sequence orientation must be checked against figure before reuse
- **Do not equate title and experiment:** modeled single-interface average R=0.54% over 8–13 µm; fabrication added 10 nm Y2O3 adhesion and reoptimized (model 0.62%). Double-sided fabricated Si showed average R=3.31% at normal incidence, versus 1.09% double-sided model
- **Conditions:** Si 500±30 µm; both faces coated; deposited at 120°C. FTIR measured normal→70°; normal-incidence performance substantially retained to 50°. Figure 5a design thickness and Figure 5b TEM thickness are distinct prescriptions
- **Absent:** retrieved full reoptimized/TEM layer list, polarized performance and thermal-cycle qualification. Main text explicitly raises possible thermal-mismatch delamination

## Reuse guidance

These cases span AR, dichroic, metal HR, dielectric HR and PBS with actual measured comparators. Only the thermal AR table and protected-Ag recipe are immediately unambiguous substrate-outward physical prescriptions. The dichroic's disclosed table belongs only to its rear AR. Literature recipes are not qualified manufacturing instructions: deposited n,k, density, adhesion and back-surface treatment must remain part of each comparison. Record missing parameters explicitly rather than filling them with nominal handbook data without labeling the substitution.
