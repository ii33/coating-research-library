1. Characteristic-matrix optics

Input: substrate-outward positive-thickness nm layers, explicit tabulated n/k, wavelength in nm. Output: intrinsic normal-incidence R/T and coating A. Convert layers to air-to-substrate order. Interpolate n and k separately linearly; reject outside the full actual source domain. Implementation: scripts/verify_prescriptions.py, Material.sample and response.

N=n+i*k; delta=2*pi*N*d/lambda
Cj=[[cos(delta),-i*sin(delta)/N],[-i*N*sin(delta),cos(delta)]]
C=ordered product Cj; D=C11+C12*Ns+C21+C22*Ns
r=(C11+C12*Ns-C21-C22*Ns)/D; t=2/D
R=abs(r)^2; T=real(Ns)*abs(t)^2; A=1-R-T

Figure1: physical propagation is air-to-substrate, opposite to stored layer numbering. Passive scalar isotropic plane-parallel coherent layers only. T enters the final half-space; A excludes semi-infinite substrate absorption. No oblique-angle implementation is claimed for this script. Thick lossy stacks may make matrices ill-conditioned; the present prescriptions were crosschecked against the original frozen scattering recurrence. Derivation reference: S. J. Byrnes, Multilayer optical calculations.

2. Dense/shifted/source-knot/local-extremum audit

Input: prescription and targets.json; output: each window's Rmax/Tmin/Amax with wavelength and pass/fail. Default:10001 uniform points plus10000 half-step points per window plus every source-table knot. Extremum brackets are adjacent sampled peaks/valleys. Solve each in dimensionless t using the independent golden-section implementation bounded_minimum, t tolerance1e-12, maximum500 iterations, including bracket endpoints. The expected-vs-recomputed power-metric tolerance is2e-9. Implementation: audit and bounded_minimum in the standalone script.

lambda=left+(right-left)*t, t in[0,1]
minimize -R(t), T(t), -A(t) in each bracket
pass iff Rmax<=0.0099+1e-6 and Tmin>=0.9001-1e-6

Figure2: dense and shifted samples plus source-table folds locate local brackets; local normalized coordinates keep wavelength units out of the scalar tolerance. These finite grids, bracket rules and numerical settings are project research conventions, not a mathematical continuous certificate. Unimodal golden-section assumption applies inside each detected bracket; undiscovered very narrow extrema remain a known limitation. No external source was found validating this project-specific audit combination; it is only this project's implementation. The local matrix functions were independently checked against the previous NumPy/SciPy audit and frozen core to roundoff.
