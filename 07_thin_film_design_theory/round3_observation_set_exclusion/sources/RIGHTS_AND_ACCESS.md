# Sources and rights

The public primary-source links and exact use limits are in source_registry.json. The publisher abstract for the 1993 paper was available in search; its full-text page did not open. No claim relies on having read that full article. SIVIA definitions were additionally read in an author-hosted original paper, section III.A–B.

Original PDFs are held separately in sources/originals, excluded from the reader manifest, ZIP and source-file list. The local archive receipt is also outside the reader payload. Those files retain their authors' and publishers' rights. Public access is not redistribution permission. The reports, figures and new algorithm code in this package are original; unchanged local reference dependencies are hash-bound, with their inherited provenance preserved.

No account identifier, credential, private browser URL or private communication is a source. Real material constants and calibration evidence were not obtained in this task.
